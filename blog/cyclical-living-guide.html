Why Free and Privacy-First Period Tracking Matters

Published August 22, 2026 · 5 min read

Most period tracking apps charge 10–20 USD per month for basic features — and many of them sell or share your health data with third parties. Luna Sync was built to prove that free, private, and beautiful can coexist. Here's why that matters.

The Hidden Cost of "Free" Period Apps

The most popular period tracking apps are free to download, but they monetize through premium subscriptions (often $10–20/month), medical advertising, and data sharing with analytics companies. Some apps have been found sharing cycle data with Facebook and other advertisers — data that reveals pregnancy, fertility intentions, and health conditions.

After the overturning of Roe v. Wade in the United States, period tracking data became a legal liability. Law enforcement has subpoenaed cycle data from apps to prosecute individuals. This is not a hypothetical risk — it has happened.

Why Local-First Privacy Is Non-Negotiable

Local-first means all your data lives on your device — not on a company's server. When you log a period, track symptoms, or view your moon phase visualization, the computation happens on your iPhone. No data ever leaves your phone.

Luna Sync takes this further: no servers, no cloud accounts, no analytics SDKs, no telemetry. We don't just promise privacy — we architect for it. You can use Luna Sync entirely offline after the initial download.
